\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\begin{DoxyVerb}Convert a ``PIL Image`` to a tensor of the same type. This transform does not support torchscript.

Converts a PIL Image (H x W x C) to a Tensor of shape (C x H x W).
\end{DoxyVerb}
