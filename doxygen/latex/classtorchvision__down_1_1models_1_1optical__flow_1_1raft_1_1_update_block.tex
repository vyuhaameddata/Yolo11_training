\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\begin{DoxyVerb}The update block which contains the motion encoder, the recurrent block, and the flow head.

It must expose a ``hidden_state_size`` attribute which is the hidden state size of its recurrent block.
\end{DoxyVerb}
